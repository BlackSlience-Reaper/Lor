[blue][b]NEW CONTENT[/b][/blue]

- Added Hokma as an Act 3 floor and Religion Floor Liberation (Paradise Lost). Paradise Lost fights alongside the Scythe, Spear, and Staff Apostles, with Awe's card sacrifice and reclamation, apostle feigned death and awakening, and endings including The Time Has Come and Atonement.
- Added Act 4, The Reverberation Ensemble, with its own opening event, map, and music. Three rounds of floor receptions alternate with supplies; each supply row offers a Shop, Rest Site, or Treasure.
- Added the History Floor's uninvited guests, The Crying Children: Philip and the Unspeaking Children, featuring three phases, overheating, Burn amplification, and burning cards in hand.
- Added the Technological Sciences Floor's uninvited guests, Church of Gears: Eileen and the Church of Gears Followers, featuring Smoke, Lucid Smoke, reinforcements, and phase HP floors. Other floor reception nodes currently use existing encounter pools, and the final node uses the existing Act 3 vanilla Boss pool.
- Added Alriune and One Born of Dust to the Act 2 normal encounter pool. Attacking Alriune consumes Crown of Atonement; Staggering it activates Tears of a Flower, reducing its HP and replenishing crowns.
- Added individual three-section map backgrounds for History, Technological Sciences, Literature, Art, Language, Natural Sciences, Social Sciences, Philosophy, and Religion, with matching route, node, and floor emblem colors.

[blue][b]PAGE BALANCE[/b][/blue]

- All-Around Helper Page, Clean: now deals damage and Stagger damage equal to your hand size to all enemies after each turn starts, replacing the direct HP loss triggered by the first Attack card played.
- Happy Teddy Page, Longing Embrace: now also grants 3 permanent Slash Power Up at combat start; Block gained when breaking existing Block increased from 7 to 8.
- Heart of Aspiration Page, Pulse: its 4 Power Up now lasts for the entire combat instead of 1 turn.
- Burrowing Heaven Page, Withering Blood Wings: reflected damage increased from 70% to 100%.
- Little Red Mercenary Page, Prey: now marks the first target dealt unblocked attack damage each turn. Pets can also mark targets and benefit from the bonus damage, which increased from 3 to 4.
- Queen Bee Page, Spore: Burn and Bleed increased from 6 to 8 each. Loyalty: accumulated HP loss required per trigger reduced from 5 to 4; Power Up duration increased from 1 to 2 turns.
- Magic Bullet Shooter Page, Seventh Bullet: damage reduction against yourself and other players increased from 75% to 80%. Black Flame now weakens both physical and Stagger Resistances.
- Ozma Page, Power of the Past: the 3x multiplier now also applies to Stagger damage. Forget: healing increased from 30% to 50% of Max HP. Powder of Life: healing increased from 11 to 14.
- Fairy Festival Page, Predation: combat-start HP loss now excludes pets.
- Fragment from Somewhere EGO Page: Strength and Dexterity reductions reduced from 3 to 2 each.

[blue][b]ENCOUNTER AND LIBERATION BALANCE[/b][/blue]

[b]The overall focus is on reducing difficulty so that vanilla characters can play normally. Future difficulty additions are planned through the Second Ascension.[/b]

- Queen of Hatred: normal HP reduced from 335–340 to 305–310, and high Ascension HP from 440–450 to 313–315; several attacks deal less damage. Inversion gains at end of turn, upon HP loss, and on a fully blocked attack reduced from 6, 6, and 17 to 3, 3, and 12. After transforming, fully restores HP.
- Big Bad Wolf: normal HP reduced from 490–498 to 430–438, and high Ascension HP from 502–505 to 452–455.
- Followers in the Blue Star encounter: HP reduced from 500 to 200 and Stagger Resistance from 120 to 100. Return to the Stars' Embrace now resolves Staggered or dead Followers at end of turn and damages the Blue Star Altar for a percentage of its Max HP.
- Queen Bee, Spores: each player's first unblocked attack each turn can now grant 1 Energy. Spores next turn only affect the attackers who actually dealt that damage. Reduced Block from Queen Bee's buff and vigilance moves.
- Scarecrow Searching for Wisdom, Harvest Wisdom: interval reduced from 3 to 2 turns, rotating through the middle, left, and right positions. Healing reduced from 12% to 10%, applied once after the full attack if it hit. Removed bonus damage against an empty Draw Pile and the Peace of Obtained Wisdom and Empty Head passives.
- Warmhearted Woodsman: reworked Warm Heart and Violent Heart. Each Warm Heart stack restores 1 Energy to every player at the start of the player turn. Attacks deal double damage to trees; killing a tree restores 10% Max HP and grants 4 Strength and 2 Warm Heart. Logging now hits three times and targets a living tree first. Empty Heart is used on turn 1 and every 6 turns thereafter.
- Ozma, Take or Be Taken: progress on hitting the real Jack is shared by all players, requiring 8, 10, 12, or 15 hits with 1, 2, 3, or 4 players. Failure costs all Forgotten players 50% of their current HP.
- Fairy Festival: Fairy Queen's low-HP enhancement threshold increased from 25% to 40%. Queen's Decree Block reduced from 9 to 7; Predation healing and Block reduced from 6 and 10 to 4 and 9. Fairy Mass's Wingbeat damage per hit reduced from 4 (5 on high Ascension) to 3 (4).
- Scorched Girl, Fourth Match Flame: self-damage increased from 10 to 15.
- Reduced HP for enemies including All-Around Helper, Galaxy Friend, and Hermit of the Azure Forest. Reduced damage and secondary effects on several Cosmic Fragment, Today's Shy Look, Forgotten Knight Sword, and Red Shoes moves; increased Big Bird's high Ascension HP.
- History Floor, Emerald Bough: reduced HP and vine pressure. Emerald Bough's Vulnerable reduced from 9 stacks for 3 turns to 6 stacks for 2 turns; Grudge Vine adds 1 Wound instead of 2, and Vine Barrier's Block reduced from 20 to 12.
- History Floor, Fluttering: Fresh Meat now increases incoming Fluttering attack damage by 25% and lets Fluttering heal for the unblocked damage dealt. Momentary Satiety now restores 10% Max HP and grants 4 Vigor; Hunger now restores 20% Max HP and permanently grants 3 Strength.
- Technological Sciences Floor, Grinder Mk4: charge required for Limiter Release increased from 3 to 4. Solemn Mourning: sealed cards playable per turn reduced from 6 to 4.
- Art Floor, Incomprehensible: own turns without dealing unblocked damage required to trigger the EGO Page increased from 2 to 3.
- Social Sciences Floor Liberation: opening healing reduced from full HP to 25% of Max HP.
- Natural Sciences Floor: Magical Girl encounters now appear more often. King of Greed's encounter weight increased to 2x; Queen of Hatred, Despair Knight, and The Servant of Wrath weights increased to 1.5x.

[blue][b]SECOND ASCENSION REWORK PLAN[/b][/blue]

- Canard (level 1): enemy Stagger Resistance caps now use player-count multipliers of 1, 1.1, 1.3, and 1.5 for 1, 2, 3, and 4+ players.
- Urban Myth (level 2): the effect disabling HP recovery between Liberation phases now begins at this level.
- Urban Legend (level 3): added enemy Max HP multipliers of 1, 1.1, 1.4, and 1.6 for 1, 2, 3, and 4+ players. Removed the previous random physical and Stagger Resistance upgrades.
- Urban Plague (level 4): Stagger recovery per turn changed from a flat 8% of the cap to 1%, 1%, 2%, or 3%, based on player count.
- Reverberation Ensemble (level 8): lose 10% of Max HP at run start, rounded down with a minimum loss of 1; Max HP remains at least 1.

[blue][b]STAGGER RESISTANCE BAR STYLE[/b][/blue]

- Added Default and Badge choices under the mod settings' Combat UI → Stagger Resistance bar style. Badge is selected initially; this option does not affect multiplayer.
- Default: a separate Stagger bar above the HP bar, with current and maximum values centered inside it.
- Badge (thanks again to [blue]Natsuki[/blue] for the design!): the yellow Stagger bar sits along the HP bar's top edge, with the current value in a gold hexagonal badge at the right end. Physical and Stagger Resistance icons sit above the badge, which turns red while Staggered.
