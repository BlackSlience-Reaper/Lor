Library of Ruina v0.18.9 is now available! The following notes compare directly against v0.18.7, covering stronger Abnormality Pages, adjustments to Dead Butterflies and Twilight, Floor Liberation fixes, and a persistent update-log entry built from the native game screen.

[blue][b]CONTENT & BALANCE:[/b][/blue]

[b]Abnormality Pages:[/b]
- Buffed [b]Big Bird Page[/b]:
  - Watchful Eye cooldown decreased from 3 turns → 2 turns
  - Ever-Burning Lamp cooldown decreased from 7 turns → 6 turns
  - Salvation permanent Strong increased from 2 → 3
- Buffed [b]Blue Star Page[/b]:
  - Martyrdom Stagger damage increased from 4(6) → 7(9)
  - Atonement Stagger damage and additional HP-loss bonus increased from 10% → 15%
- Buffed [b]Burrowing Heaven Page[/b]:
  - Withering Blood Wings recoil increased from 30% → 70%
  - Another's Gaze extra draw increased from 3 → 4 and next-turn Energy increased from 2 → 3
  - Attention and Focus damage and Stagger damage bonus increased from 30% → 40%
- Buffed [b]Heart of Aspiration Page[/b]:
  - Pulse permanent Strong increased from 3 → 4
  - Aspiration max HP bonus increased from 33% → 40%
  - Violent Pulse Strong, Endurance, Protection, and Quickness increased from 5 → 8
- Buffed [b]Judgement Bird Page[/b]: Tilted Scale healing increased from 2 → 3 and its per-turn trigger limit increased from 3 → 4
- Buffed [b]Ozma Page[/b]: Power of the Past Attack damage multiplier increased from 2x → 3x
- Buffed [b]Price of Silence Page[/b]:
  - Time Energy and extra draw both increased from 1 → 2
  - The Thirteenth Toll draw increased from 1 → 2
  - Silence Weak increased from 24 → 48
- Buffed [b]Punishing Bird Page[/b]:
  - Punishment HP-loss multiplier increased from 8x → 9x
  - Punitive Beak Strength increased from 2 → 5
- Buffed [b]Scarecrow Page[/b]:
  - Harvest additional attack and Stagger damage increased from 9 → 10
  - Torn-Out Wisdom interval decreased from 7 cards → 6 cards and its Energy increased from 1 → 2
- Buffed [b]Warmhearted Woodsman Page[/b]:
  - Warm Heart permanent Strong increased from 2 → 3
  - Heart HP-loss threshold decreased from 24 → 20
  - Logging Strong cap increased from 6 → 8

[b]Enemies:[/b]
- Changed [b]Dead Butterflies[/b]:
  - Stagger Resistance decreased from 20 → 10
  - Peaceful Repose now hits 2 times instead of 3, with each hit dealing 1 more damage
- Changed [b]Twilight[/b]:
  - Small Beak now increases the cost of one random card in hand by 2 for the turn whenever a card is played
  - Long Arms now reflects prevented debuffs to their applier
  - Prowl Block decreased from 16 → 12, Tilted Scale Sin decreased from 12 → 9, and Comeuppance healing per Sin decreased from 10% → 6%
  - Selected damage values were reduced for Slam Down, Talon, Let’s Protect the Black Forest, Tilted Scale, Comeuppance, and Peace for All
- Changed [b]False Throne[/b]: Playtime is Over!!! now applies 999 Weak for 1 turn and no longer applies Disarm

[b]Floor Liberations:[/b]
- Buffed [b]Literature Floor Liberation[/b]: Obsession Soulbound Chains increased from 1 → 2
- Changed Su~per~ Present!!, I Can't Stand It!!!, and Swan Song: when attack damage exceeds the target's Block, half of that Block is removed before the group attack resolves
- Existing normal Bleed is now preserved when entering the Bloodlust phase

[blue][b]USER EXPERIENCE & INTERFACE:[/b][/blue]

- Added a [b]View Update Log[/b] entry to mod settings using the game's native patch-notes screen, scrollbar, paging, and back controls
- Floor Liberation Boss map icons now use the traveled, untraveled, and outline colors of their respective floors
- Custom main-menu and non-combat BGM relative volume is now consistently scaled to 60%
- Lower-tier Floor Liberation settlement text now displays the number of randomly upgraded cards

[blue][b]BUG FIXES:[/b][/blue]

[b]Combat:[/b]
- Fixed [b]Big Bird[/b] failing to enter Stagger correctly before Asleep expires; it now empties Stagger Resistance and preserves the next awake move
- Fixed [b]Little Red Riding Hooded Mercenary[/b] and [b]Scarlet Scar[/b] not being treated as friendly allies while they are not enraged
- Fixed Smiling Bodies Page choice cards calculating HP-derived values before receiving their owner, which caused zero-value previews

[b]Writing:[/b]
- Fixed several [b]Twilight[/b] power descriptions using stale fixed values; they now read the live cycle, egg, cost, Sin, Paralysis, and HP-floor values
