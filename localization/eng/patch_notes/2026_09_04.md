Library of Ruina v0.18.10 is now available!

This page records v0.18.10 in full and summarizes the major changes from v0.18.3 through v0.18.9. The previous page retains the complete v0.18.9 value list.

[blue][b]v0.18.10: ABNORMALITY PAGE ADJUSTMENTS[/b][/blue]

- Reworked the [b]Queen of Hatred Page[/b]: Justice now makes the Villain take 50% more damage and Stagger damage; Hatred now grants 1 Strong for 3 turns per trigger, up to twice per turn
- Changed the [b]Red Shoes Page[/b]: Glitter and Axe grant permanent Strong at combat start; Bloodthirst now doubles both damage and Stagger damage
- Changed the [b]Song Machine Page[/b]: Addiction grants 2 permanent Strong and removes 1 Dexterity at combat start
- Buffed the [b]Spider Bud Page[/b]: Feeding healing increased from 10 to 12; Vigilance now gives all enemies 5 permanent Vulnerability and Stagger Vulnerability
- Buffed the [b]Spiny Bus Page[/b] and [b]Page of Nosferatu[/b]: Thorns adds 5 damage and Stagger damage to Pierce attacks; Vampirism adds 5 damage and Stagger damage against Bleeding targets
- Changed the [b]Page of the Servant of Wrath[/b]: killing a Companion now grants 1 Energy to every player, including the Page owner
- Changed the [b]Nothing There Page[/b]: Shell now prevents HP loss caused by negative effects; cards and monster moves resolve normally
- Changed the [b]Heart of Aspiration Page[/b]: Aspiration maximum HP bonus decreased from 40% to 30%

[blue][b]v0.18.10: TWILIGHT AND COMBAT RULES[/b][/blue]

- [b]Twilight[/b]'s Long Arms now removes and prevents debuffs, gaining 1 permanent Strong whenever a debuff is applied
- Prowl Strong increased from 2 to 3, and Surveillance Strength increased from 2 to 4
- When an HP lock truncates Power-origin damage, it now deals additional unblockable Stagger damage equal to 25% of the effective pre-lock damage, rounded up with a minimum of 1
- Fixed Seventh Bullet expanding its targets again during all-target damage resolution, keeping its indiscriminate target set stable

[blue][b]v0.18.10: FLOOR LIBERATION SETTLEMENT REWARDS[/b][/blue]

- Tier 1: 50 Gold, 2 Boss card rewards, 1 Common relic, the matching Abnormality Page, +10 Max HP, and 1 random card upgrade
- Tier 2: 60 Gold, 2 Boss card rewards, 1 Common relic, the matching Abnormality Page, +5 Max HP, and 2 random card upgrades
- Tier 3: 200 Gold, 1 Boss card reward, 1 Uncommon relic, the matching Abnormality Page, +8 Max HP, and 2 random card upgrades
- Tier 4: 120 Gold, 1 Boss card reward, 1 Rare relic, the matching Abnormality Page, +10 Max HP, and 3 random card upgrades

- [gold]v0.18.3–v0.18.7[/gold]: added the five-phase Literature Floor Liberation, Judgement Bird Elite, Blue Star Strong encounter, and seven Abnormality Pages, with broad Act 2–3 enemy, Page, and settlement reward tuning
- [gold]v0.18.8[/gold]: adjusted Dead Butterflies' Stagger Resistance and Peaceful Repose hit count, and refreshed the in-game version notes
- [gold]v0.18.9[/gold]: strengthened the Big Bird, Blue Star, Burrowing Heaven, Heart of Aspiration, Judgement Bird, Ozma, Price of Silence, Punishing Bird, Scarecrow, and Warmhearted Woodsman Pages; retuned Twilight, Dead Butterflies, False Throne, and the Literature Floor Liberation; added the permanent update-log entry and floor-colored Liberation Boss map icons
