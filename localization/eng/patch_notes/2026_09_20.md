[blue][b]NEW: COMBAT DAMAGE PREVIEW[/b][/blue]

- Dragging an Attack card onto an enemy, or hovering a Speed Die with a card loaded, now shows the expected damage and Stagger damage against that target, accounting for its resistances, Block, and current status.
- Multi-hit dice are listed hit by hit; each later hit uses the target's state after the previous one.
- Press Alt to cycle between Simple, Detailed, and Off. The detailed view lists every step of the calculation and can be scrolled with the mouse wheel.
- Status names in card, relic, power, and intent text now show their matching status icon.

[blue][b]NEW: VANILLA CHARACTER REWARDS[/b][/blue]

- Added a "Vanilla Character Rewards" option to the mod settings, enabled by default. When on, the Ironclad, Silent, Regent, Necrobinder, and Defect permanently gain 2 potion slots and receive 2 random potions upon entering the Library in Act 1.
- In multiplayer, the host's setting applies to everyone.

[blue][b]TWILIGHT: SECOND ASCENSION[/b][/blue]

- With the Second Ascension enabled, breaking Twilight's eggs has additional effects: breaking the Big Eyes egg removes all buffs from every player; after the Small Beak egg breaks, every player card costs at least 2 Energy; after the Long Arms egg breaks, the first 3 debuffs Twilight receives each turn are reflected back to their applier.
- The Broken Egg description shows the enhanced effects while the Second Ascension is active.

[blue][b]PAGE AND ENCOUNTER BALANCE[/b][/blue]

- Road Home Page, Courage: temporary Max HP reduced from 50% to 25%, total uses reduced from 5 to 3.
- Scarecrow Searching for Wisdom Page, Torn-Out Wisdom: cards required per trigger increased from 6 to 8.
- King of Greed: Shining Strike damage increased from 15 (16 on high Ascension) to 18 (19); Victorious Euphoria damage reduced from 18 (19) to 12 (15).
- Followers in the Blue Star encounter: Stagger Resistance reduced from 180 to 120, self-destruct threshold reduced from 20% to 10% of Stagger Resistance, and Slash, Pierce, and Blunt resistances changed from Resist to Endure.
- Bleed is no longer removed when it drops below 3 stacks; it still loses a third of its stacks on each attack and at end of turn.

[blue][b]COMBAT FIXES[/b][/blue]

- All "minimum HP" effects now resolve after every other damage modifier: Cobalt Scar, False Throne, Fairy Care, Flickering Desire, Dipsia, Nosferatu, Twilight's Fear and Eternal Peace, the Hermit of the Azure Forest, Road Home, the Wedge Office's Perseverance, and guest characters. Extra damage from Vulnerable and similar effects can no longer push through the floor, and damage previews match the actual result.
- Fixed the Forgotten Knight Sword, Cobalt Scar, Dipsia, Smiling Face, In the Name of Love and Hatred, Sword Sharpened with Tears, Gold Rush, Blind Rage, and Galaxy Friend failing to revive or advance phases while Staggered, which could stall the fight.
- Fixed Staggered enemies still switching intents.
- Fixed the King of Greed and Gold Rush endlessly repeating the same group attack after it missed and caused Stagger, instead of continuing to their next move once recovered.
- Fixed the King of Greed's Magical Girl form becoming completely immune slightly above 50% HP when its max HP was odd, with no HP lock shown and no transformation triggered; the HP floor is now rounded up to a whole number, keeping at least 50% HP.
- Fixed the Magic Bullet Shooter Page's Commission bonus being applied twice, dealing too much damage to the Commission Target.
- Fixed Cobalt Scar, Mimicry, Smiling Face, and the Mountain of Smiling Bodies dropping back to single-player Stagger Resistance after a form change in multiplayer.
- Fixed other cards and potions being unable to target players while the Forest Keeper's Lock exists; the lock now restricts only its own target.
- Fixed attack intents of this mod's allied units (companions, Magical Girls) not showing their target line.
- Queen Bee's Spores now shows the Death's Embrace trigger threshold on the HP bar.
- Several enemies' form and phase mechanics are no longer treated as buffs, so buff-removal effects no longer strip them.
- Unified the Chinese names of Disarm and Weak (no change in English).

[blue][b]STABILITY AND DISPLAY[/b][/blue]

- Encounter order is now consistent for the same seed, fixing multiplayer games where players saw different encounters.
- The battlefield rearranges when enemies are summoned or a phase adds new ones, so enemies no longer end up off-screen.
- Fixed leaving combat during a heavy hit effect potentially stalling the fight or throwing errors.
- Fixed a possible crash when playing background music in some environments.
- Fixed sound effects being cut off early when combat is sped up.
