Library of Ruina v0.19.9 Update Notes

Cumulative changes in v0.19.7–v0.19.9 since the previous v0.19.6 notes.

[blue][b]MUSIC AND SOUND EFFECTS[/b][/blue]

- Added a collapsible audio section for main-menu BGM, out-of-combat BGM (excluding the main menu), and mod sound effects. Each has its own toggle and 0%–100% volume slider; changes apply immediately and are saved.
- Main-menu and out-of-combat BGM retain their 60% default volume. Existing mod sound-effect volume settings are preserved.
- Before starting, resuming, or changing BGM, the mod stops vanilla music and identifiable music from other mods to reduce overlapping playback. Sound effects, dialogue, and our track crossfades are preserved.

[blue][b]COMBAT FIXES AND COMPATIBILITY[/b][/blue]

- Fixed Spider Bud and its Literature realization counterpart changing intent after players end their turn when a Small Spider dies. Kills while players can still act trigger immediately; deaths from poison or other effects after turn end are deferred until the next player turn starts, preserving the current enemy action's intent.
- The Literature counterpart's hunting window and presentation are deferred together. Updated all four languages and saved the pending-hunt state.
- Fixed a possible Android exception when the Focus of Attention compatibility patch reads a method without an IL body.

[blue][b]LITERATURE BALANCE[/b][/blue]

- Wee Witch's Friend: base HP reduced from 75 to 70; high-Ascension first-move damage per hit reduced from 9 to 8.
- Left Shoe: normal base HP reduced from 100–110 to 85–90, and high-Ascension HP from 115–120 to 95–100. High-Ascension Whispering Desire damage per hit reduced from 8 to 7, Bleed applied from 4 to 3, and Hidden Desire Block from 14 to 11.
- Bloodlust: high-Ascension Persistence damage per hit reduced from 8 to 7, Obsession from 20 to 17, I Can't Stand It!!! repeated-hit damage from 5 to 4, and its finisher's base damage from 16 to 15. The finisher's Bleed-stack damage multiplier reduced from 4 to 2 at all Ascensions.

[blue][b]REALIZATION SETTLEMENT REWARDS[/b][/blue]

- Standard tier 1: base Gold increased from 50 to 60, Boss card reward sets changed from 2 to 1, and Common relics increased from 1 to 2.
- Standard tier 2: base Gold changed from 60 to 10, Common relics from 2 to 1, maximum-HP gain from 5 to 1, and random card upgrades from 2 to 1.
- Standard tier 3: base Gold increased from 200 to 250; maximum-HP gain changed from 8 to 5. Tier 4 and floor-page rewards retain their existing rules; Gold remains subject to the poverty Ascension reduction.


Library of Ruina v0.19.6 Update Notes

[blue][b]v0.19.6: COMBAT FIXES[/b][/blue]

- Fixed defeat ending the run after clearing at least one Natural Sciences realization phase. Lethal damage to the last surviving player now leaves them at 1 HP and leads to the settlement event, with rewards based on completed phases.
- Fixed The Mountain of Smiling Bodies ignoring multiplayer HP scaling on entry and when changing forms.
- Fixed multiplayer desync in the Little Red battle caused by mod load order making Focus of Attention select different attack targets on each peer.
- Fixed multiplayer HP scaling for Nihil and the statues. Magical-girl allies retain fixed HP.
- Corrected Blind Rage's friendly ally classification and adjusted Nihil's Wrath-form visuals and attack position.

[blue][b]v0.19.6: FLOOR OF NATURAL SCIENCES ADJUSTMENTS[/b][/blue]

- Nihil attacks that previously targeted one player now target all players. Updated intent descriptions and target lines accordingly.
- In the Name of Love and Hatred: human-form base HP increased by 50 and serpent-form base HP by 100. In the Name of Love and Hatred damage increased from 11/13 to 14/16; high-Ascension With Love! and In the Name of Justice! deal 1 more damage per hit.
- Blind Rage: the cumulative HP-loss threshold for Sinner increased from 40 to 80. The special scream grants 2 permanent Strong instead of 3.
- Hermit's staves: base HP reduced by 10.
- Nihil: Heart-Piercing Sword damage reduced from 19/21 to 15/17, Heart-Splitting Sword from 15/17 to 11/12, and Heart-Destroying Sword from 12/13 to 7/8. Pairs indicate normal/high-Ascension damage per hit.

[blue][b]v0.19.6: OTHER BALANCE CHANGES[/b][/blue]

- Galaxy Friend: high-Ascension base HP reduced from 190–200 to 160–170; Chaos resistance reduced from 150 to 120.
- Wolf in Her Nightmares: high-Ascension base HP reduced from 725–730 to 625–630.
- Hermit of the Azure Forest: high-Ascension base HP reduced from 446–450 to 406–410; Chaos resistance reduced from 200 to 180.
- Queen of Hatred Page+: Love healing increased from 6 to 10; Hatred now grants 3 Strong instead of 2.
- Spider Bud Page: Vigilance now applies 4 permanent Vulnerable and Break Vulnerable instead of 5.


Library of Ruina v0.19.5 is now available!

This log covers cumulative changes after v0.18.11 through v0.19.5.

[blue][b]v0.19.5: FLOOR OF NATURAL SCIENCES REALIZATION[/b][/blue]

- Added the [b]Floor of Natural Sciences[/b] to Act 2 floor selection, with its realization as the Boss encounter
- Completed five phases: [b]In the Name of Love and Hatred[/b], [b]Blind Rage[/b], [b]Sword Sharpened with Tears[/b], [b]Gold Rush[/b], and hidden Boss [b]Nihil[/b] (special rewards are still in development)
- Improved transformation, Hysteria, and phase transitions; Blind Rage includes the Hermit, his staves, death resolution, revival, and Block transfer
- Added Sword Sharpened with Tears and Forgotten Knight Swords
- Added the Gold Rush phase
- Added the Nihil final battle; allies act in a fixed order and support Block transfer
- Improved phase transitions, fake death, untargetability, summon cleanup, and saved-state restoration; corrected lingering moves and sword/staff states
- Added phase backgrounds, dialogue, BGM, sound effects, character animations, and sword attack videos

[blue][b]v0.19.5: JESTER OF NIHIL PAGE AND ENHANCED PAGES[/b][/blue]

- Added [b]Jester of Nihil Page[/b] with three choices: Magical Girls, Emptiness, and Nihility
- [b]Magical Girls[/b]: obtain and enhance all four Hatred, Greed, Wrath, and Despair pages, choosing an effect for each
- [b]Emptiness[/b]: add [b]All Returns to Void[/b] to your deck; this Power costs 2, or 1 when upgraded, and lets you discard any number of cards at turn start and draw that many, gaining 2 Strong and 2 Endurance per discarded card for 1 turn
- [b]Nihility[/b]: requires all four magical-girl pages; right-click to toggle, applying 9 Weak, Disarm, Vulnerable, and Break Vulnerable to all enemies for 1 turn, at most once per turn and 9 times per combat
- [b]Queen of Hatred Page+[/b]: Love heals 6 HP whenever you break enemy Block; Justice marks 1 random Bad Guy for 75% increased damage and Chaos damage taken; Hatred grants 2 Strong for 2 turns whenever you lose HP
- [b]King of Greed Page+[/b]: Intemperance stuns all enemies for 1 turn every 6 turns; Road to Happiness grants 1 Protection for 1 turn per unblocked damage event, up to 3; Greed grants 40% lifesteal on unblocked attack damage
- [b]Servant of Wrath Page+[/b]: Wrath grants 3 Energy, 3 extra cards, and 5 permanent Strong at combat start, with indiscriminate random and group attacks for the first 3 turns; killing your Friend grants other players 3 Energy and 3 cards; Venom applies 10 Corrosion to all enemies at combat start
- [b]Knight of Despair Page+[/b]: Blessing grants 3 permanent Protection after the first unblocked attack received; Despair grants all players 5 permanent Strong and 3 permanent Protection after the first enemy death; Sword of Tears enchants 2 Attack cards with Sharp 6 and adds damage equal to 5% of maximum HP on unblocked hits

[blue][b]v0.19.5: BALANCE AND SETTLEMENT REWARDS[/b][/blue]

- [b]Magic Bullet Shooter Page[/b]: Request damage bonus reduced from 50% to 30%, kill reward reduced from 75 to 50 Gold; The Seventh Bullet now deals 50% less damage to yourself and other players
- Standard realization tiers 2–4 now award 2 regular reward relics instead of 1; tier 1 remains at 1, with the floor page awarded separately
- Tier 1: 50 Gold, 2 Boss card rewards, 1 Common relic, the floor page, +10 maximum HP, and 1 random card upgrade
- Tier 2: 60 Gold, 2 Boss card rewards, 2 Common relics, the floor page, +5 maximum HP, and 2 random card upgrades
- Tier 3: 200 Gold, 1 Boss card reward, 2 Uncommon relics, the floor page, +8 maximum HP, and 2 random card upgrades
- Tier 4: 120 Gold, 1 Boss card reward, 2 Rare relics, the floor page, +10 maximum HP, and 3 random card upgrades; Gold follows the poverty Ascension reduction, and the Natural Sciences special tier uses tier 4's base rewards

[blue][b]v0.19.5: COMBAT AND PRESENTATION FIXES[/b][/blue]

- Corrected Chaos-state checks across monsters, allies, and passives; creatures keep their Hit pose while staggered and return to their current form's Idle pose on recovery
- Fixed shared Strength growth between [b]Fragment of the Universe[/b] instances, guarded doubling against overflow, and stopped follow-up attacks and cleared Epiphany tracking after death
- Fixed lingering moves and statuses on special-guest hands after fake death; adjusted special-guest state creation to avoid unrelated writes during new runs and loading
- Improved Philosophy realization CG queuing, exit cleanup, and background-effect lifetimes; corrected coverage of delayed backgrounds and lingering transition visuals
- Fixed the first page-reward tutorial opening after the rewards screen closed, and improved cleanup of UI, animations, and filters
