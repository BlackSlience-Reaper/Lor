Library of Ruina v0.19.17 Update Notes

Changes in v0.19.16–v0.19.17 since the previous v0.19.15 notes.

[blue][b]COMBAT AND ALLY TARGETING[/b][/blue]

- Fixed Magical Powder preventing other cards from targeting teammates while present in combat. Its targeting restriction now applies only to itself, for both mouse and controller selection.
- Unified friend-or-foe checks for Magical Girls and other friendly companions. Fixed enemy-targeted attacks, debuffs, marks, and various Abnormality Page effects affecting allies, including checks after an ally dies.
- Fixed Power Nullification blocking damage from other sources. Cards and monster moves resolve using their base values and resistances.
- Mass attacks now round down the amount of Block removed when breaking half of it, and skip the pause when no Block is actually removed.
- Fixed the last Hermit's Staff dying replacing the move already displayed. The current move is preserved while its intent, target line, and actual targets update together.
- Optimized combined and counter intent animations to reduce repeated calculations and texture updates.

[blue][b]NATURAL SCIENCES AND PAGES[/b][/blue]

- Damage and Stagger damage to Nihil from non-Magical Girl sources are now capped at 1 per hit.
- HP loss upon meeting the Greed, Hatred, Despair, and Wrath phase conditions changed from a flat 50 to 5% of maximum HP, accounting for multiplayer HP scaling.
- Justice's three heart-protection sword attacks now inflict additional HP loss equal to 3%, 5%, and 7% of the target's maximum HP after dealing unblocked damage, replacing 30, 40, and 50 HP. Each move triggers this at most once.
- Fixed enhanced Magical Girl Pages skipping on-obtain effects when inheriting a selected effect. Tear Sword's enhanced enchantment now applies correctly and only once.
- Nihil's Page: Nihility activates at the start of each turn while enabled. Right-clicking only toggles it, with the limit of 12 activations per combat retained.
- Updated related intents and effect descriptions across Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean.

[blue][b]ENCOUNTER BALANCE[/b][/blue]

- Reduced damage on several Big Bad Wolf moves. Adjusted normal and high-Ascension damage on several Hermit of the Azure Forest moves.
- Increased the Eyeball Bird's Block in the Big Bird encounter from 15 to 25.
- Adjusted King of Greed's resistance, Strength loss, and several high-Ascension attacks. Shining Happiness gains 10 HP, its Stagger Resistance decreases from 40 to 30, and its Blunt resistances are adjusted.
- Increased some Price of Silence attack damage and some Time Trace move damage and Block.
