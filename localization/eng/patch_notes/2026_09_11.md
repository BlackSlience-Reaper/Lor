Library of Ruina v0.19.15 Update Notes

Cumulative changes in v0.19.10–v0.19.15 since the previous v0.19.9 notes.

[blue][b]v0.19.15: NIHIL ADJUSTMENTS[/b][/blue]

- Nihil's Stagger damage resistance changed from Endure to Fatal. Removed the 75 Regen and 150 Hardened Shell granted on entry.
- Reduced damage for the magic attack, all three heart-sword attacks, and Wrath-form attacks. The roar now hits twice instead of three times; the incarnation attack's next-turn Corrosion reduced from 10 to 5.
- Love, Justice, Happiness, and Courage Magical Girls' base HP increased from 60, 55, 75, and 70 to 70, 65, 80, and 85 respectively. Happiness and Courage's Stagger Resistance changed from 70 and 65 to 50 and 40.
- Increased damage for Justice's three sword attacks and their additional HP loss on breaking through Block from 10, 15, and 20 to 30, 40, and 50. Increased Happiness's Power Up for herself and her allies; her Weak application increased from 2 to 3.
- Fixed multi-hit mass attacks in this encounter so each hit finishes breaking Block and pausing before dealing damage.

[blue][b]PAGES AND EFFECTS[/b][/blue]

- Abnormality Pages with no selected effect now show all three choice cards on hover, letting you inspect their branches before claiming them.
- Nihil's Page: Magical Girls now enhances the Magical Girl Pages you already own and inherits their selected effects. Nihility's activation limit per combat increased from 9 to 12.
- Role of the Wolf grants 2 permanent Power Up at combat start. Dealing unblocked damage applies 1 permanent Vulnerable and 1 permanent Break Vulnerable, up to twice per combat.
- Fixed Corpse Absorption's permanent Power Up carrying over between combats. It now lasts for the current combat, and stale counters in older saves are cleared.
- The Knight of Despair Page's Despair and enhanced Despair+ now both buff only their holder.
- Fixed Friend marks tracking their applier, clearing previous targets, and awarding kills correctly. Friend+ now grants Energy and card draws to all players upon a qualifying kill.
- Corrected Black Flame's physical resistance conversion and description. Protection gained from Self-Intoxication now explicitly lasts 1 turn.
- The Magic Bullet's Page: Request damage bonus increased from 30% to 50%, and kill Gold from 50 to 80. Fixed Request marking another enemy after its first target dies. Seventh Bullet's damage reduction against players increased from 50% to 75%, with an indicator when it is about to activate.

[blue][b]FLOOR OF NATURAL SCIENCES[/b][/blue]

- When the act Boss is the Natural Sciences realization, King of Greed's encounter weight is doubled; Queen of Hatred, Knight of Despair, and Servant of Wrath receive 1.5 times their previous weight.
- Gold Rush: base HP increased by 100, Stagger Resistance increased from 200 to 250, and physical resistances changed to Endure. For Happiness Block increased from 33 to 55; Gluttony Block increased from 20 to 30.
- Nihil is immune to damage from sources other than Magical Girls and receives 50% fewer Doom stacks. Each form's damage reduction against non-matching Magical Girls changed from 90% to 50%, applying to both damage and Stagger damage.
- Nihil's starting Regen increased from 50 to 75, with 150 Hardened Shell added. Statue base HP reduced from 10 to 6; Love's unblocked-hit threshold reduced from 10 to 6.
- Corrected multiplayer Stagger Resistance scaling for Magical Girl allies and adjusted the Love and Hatred counters.

[blue][b]COMBAT FIXES AND BALANCE[/b][/blue]

- Fixed Spider Bud failing to switch immediately to its hunting intent while the player could still act in single-player.
- Fixed the Hermit of the Azure Forest and Servant of Wrath retaining outdated intents and targets after the last Hermit's Staff dies.
- Added HP-bar previews for Fairy Mass devouring and corrected a Stagger-recovery boundary check for Philosophy's Apocalypse Bird.
- Regular Red Shoes encounter: Left Shoe Stagger Resistance reduced from 90 to 70, Block from 15 to 10, and Strength granted to Right Shoe from 3 to 2. Right Shoe Stagger Resistance reduced from 60 to 40 and Obsession Strength from 3 to 2. Adjusted several attacks' damage.
- Forgotten Knight's Sword: high-Ascension HP reduced from 128–130 to 108–110, with several high-Ascension attacks dealing 1 less damage per hit. Sword of Grief Block increased from 17 to 19; Heart-Rending's Dazed and Heart-Ruining's Strength reduced from 3 to 2.
- Removed the outdated multiplayer-unsupported notice.

[blue][b]SHARED LIBRARY UPDATES (1.2.6–1.2.8)[/b][/blue]

- Fixed Vulnerable applying extra HP loss when the remaining damage is less than 1, aligning its trigger with the final HP-loss rounding rule.
- Targets that are already Staggered or have exhausted their Stagger Resistance no longer resolve further Stagger damage, preventing repeated Stagger triggers and related effects. Fixed a null-reference error when no valid Stagger-damage result is produced.
- Only monsters derived from the shared library's monster model use its resistance and Stagger systems. Other monsters retain their original behavior, improving compatibility with the base game and other mods.
- Card damage previews now account for the target's physical resistance, covering vanilla normal, calculated, and Osty damage as well as shared-library damage variables. Corrected group-attack preview handling when targets have different resistances.
